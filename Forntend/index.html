What We Do

Our Consultancy Areas

End-to-end fraud management and audit consulting across all claim types.

Cashless / Intimation Investigation

Real-time verification of cashless claims — confirming genuineness of hospitalization, room category, member identity, and pre-authorization before approval.

Claims Audit

Comprehensive post-claim audits to identify inflated bills, upcoding, and inconsistencies between medical records and submitted claims.

Death Claim Investigation

Thorough verification of death claims including cause of death, hospitalization history, and potential fraudulent circumstances.

Personal Accident

Investigation of accidental claims including MLC records, alcohol history verification, and relative statements to determine claim validity.

Reimbursement Claim Investigation

Field investigation of reimbursement claims — verifying hospital visits, treatment records, and supporting documents for accuracy.

Cashless Investigation

What We Verify

Every cashless claim is evaluated against a rigorous checklist to prevent fraud at the point of hospitalization.

Genuinely of Hospitalization
Check for Room Category
Verify the Member Identity
Line of Treatment as per Diagnosis
Pre-authorization Request Validation
History of Illness — Hypertension, Diabetes, etc.
History of Habits — e.g. Alcoholism
Record of First Detection of Illness / Ailment
Accidental Case — H/O Alcohol, MLC Record, Relatives Statement
